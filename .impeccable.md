## Design Context

### Users

Estonian (primary), Finnish, and English-speaking small business owners and freelancers who need a website. They are not technical — they don't know or care about frameworks. They want a fast, beautiful, trustworthy site that makes a good first impression, ranks in Google, and doesn't cause ongoing headaches. They arrive looking for someone who speaks their language (literally and figuratively) and explains things simply.

### Brand Personality

**Approachable, honest, competent.**

Voice is warm and conversational — like explaining something to a friend over coffee, not pitching from a stage. No jargon. No buzzwords. No pressure. Copy should feel human-to-human: "Kas su koduleht on aeglane?" not "Leveraging cutting-edge technology." The founder (Kenert) is a Sanalliset co-founder who built a real e-commerce business from scratch — this blend of entrepreneur + developer credibility should come through naturally.

Temperature zones:

- **Warm** — hero, about, problem descriptions, CTAs
- **Neutral** — service specs, pricing details, FAQ answers
- **Empathetic** — error states, empty states, loading

Copywrite rules: no em/en dashes as punctuation, no pushy sales language.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Elegant & refined** — generous whitespace, breathing room, warm minimalism.

- **Colors**: Custom OKLch shadcn palette (teal/cyan primary, zinc neutrals). This palette is intentionally crafted — do not change it. All UI must build on shadcn/ui tokens.
- **Typography**: Geist (sans) + Inter Body. No serif pairing. Clean, modern, consistent.
- **Border radius**: 0.625rem base with calculated scale (already defined).
- **Icons**: Lucide — warm, approachable, not corporate/industrial.
- **Theme**: Light
- **Animations**: Subtle, 200–500ms, must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Anti-references: corporate, cluttered, generic templates. This should feel like a craftsperson's studio, not a SaaS landing page.

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — Every element earns its place. If something doesn't help the visitor understand or take action, remove it.
2. **Human first** — Write and design for real people, not personas. Use language they'd use. Show faces, not stock graphics.
3. **Quiet confidence** — Let quality speak. No ALL CAPS CTAs, no countdown timers, no "LIMITED OFFER." The work and the words should build trust naturally.
4. **Progressive disclosure** — Don't overwhelm. Lead with the headline, reveal detail on demand. Pricing → packages → consultation.
5. **Accessibility is baseline** — WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reduced motion support. Semantic HTML. Keyboard navigable.

### Page Structure

Single-page portfolio with these sections (top to bottom):

1. **Hero** — One clear value proposition in plain language. Subtext with key benefit. CTA: "Broneeri tasuta konsultatsioon."
2. **Problem** — Why typical websites fail (slow, maintenance, plugin issues). Describe pain the client feels, don't bash competitors.
3. **Solution** — 3–4 concise points on what's different (speed, no maintenance, self-editable content, secure). Link to WordPress comparison article.
4. **Services & Pricing** — Three packages (landing page, business site, e-commerce) with starting prices, short descriptions, individual CTAs.
5. **Portfolio** — Completed work (Sanalliset + others). Screenshot, short description, live link.
6. **About** — Photo, name, 2–3 sentences. Entrepreneur + developer credibility.
7. **FAQ** — 4–5 common questions (content editing, timeline, hosting, migrating from WordPress).
8. **CTA / Contact** — "Valmis alustama?" + booking button + contact info.

### Tech Stack

- Next.js 16 (App Router, RSC) — check `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` for breaking changes
- React 19, TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS 4 (inline @theme, @tailwindcss/postcss)
- shadcn/ui (Radix Vega style, CVA variants) — MUST be used for all UI
- Lucide icons
- Multilingual: Estonian (primary), Finnish, English
